Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: we collect only what you type into our enquiry form, we use it only to respond to your enquiry and provide our services, we never sell it, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete it at any time by writing to experttaxcenter@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

This website, www.experttaxcenter.com, is operated by Expert Tax Center, a tax consultancy and financial guidance practice serving clients in India ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data you share with us through this website.

Contact: experttaxcenter@gmail.com

2. What personal data we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us in the enquiry form:

DataRequired?Why we need it
Full nameYesTo address you and identify your enquiry
Mobile numberYesTo call or message you about your enquiry
Email addressOptionalTo send you a confirmation and reply by email
Client type and service neededYesTo understand your situation and route your request
Approximate income or turnover rangeOptionalTo suggest the right service and estimate fees
Your query (free text)YesTo understand what help you need

Please do not enter PAN, Aadhaar, bank account numbers, passwords or tax documents in the enquiry form. If you become a client, we will collect the documents needed for your work through a secure channel agreed with you.

Technical data: our hosting provider automatically processes limited technical information (such as IP address, browser type and request logs) to deliver the website securely and protect it from abuse. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

3. How we use your personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email as needed.
  • To send you an automatic email confirming we received your enquiry (only if you gave an email address).
  • If you engage us, to provide tax filing, tax planning, GST/TDS and related services, and to meet our obligations under tax and other applicable laws.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent spam or misuse.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your separate consent.

4. Legal basis: your consent

We process your enquiry data on the basis of the consent you give by ticking the consent box before submitting the form. Where you become a client, we may also process data as needed to perform our engagement and to comply with law.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing experttaxcenter@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and we may need to keep certain records where the law requires it.

5. Who we share your data with

We share your data only with service providers who help us run this website and communicate with you (Data Processors), and only as needed for these purposes:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, security and delivery.
  • Resend: delivering enquiry and confirmation emails.
  • Google (Gmail): the email inboxes where enquiries are received, including our website administrator's inbox used to monitor that the enquiry system works correctly.

We may also disclose personal data where required by law, a court order or a government authority.

6. Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers may store or process data on servers outside India. Such transfers are permitted under the DPDP Act except to countries the Government of India may restrict, and we will follow any such restrictions.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: deleted within 12 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask.
  • Client records: kept for as long as needed to provide our services and for the periods required under income tax, GST and other applicable laws.

8. How we protect your data

The website is served only over encrypted HTTPS. Access to enquiry inboxes is limited to the people who need it to respond to you. We take reasonable security safeguards, as required by the DPDP Act, to prevent personal data breaches. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correction and update: ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with us (see below).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To use any of these rights, email experttaxcenter@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10. Grievances and complaints

Grievance contact: Grievance Officer, Expert Tax Center
Email: experttaxcenter@gmail.com (subject: "Privacy grievance")

We aim to respond to grievances as quickly as possible and in any case within the time allowed by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

This website and our enquiry form are intended for adults (18 years and above). If you are under 18, please ask a parent or lawful guardian to contact us on your behalf. If we learn we have collected a child's data without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or in the law, including as further provisions of the DPDP Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 come into effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
