Common questions

FAQ

Do I need to file a return if my tax is zero?

Often yes. Filing is needed if your gross income crosses the basic exemption limit, and it's also useful to claim TDS refunds, carry forward losses, or show income proof for loans and visas.

Old regime or new regime — which is better for me?

It depends on how much you claim in deductions (80C, 80D, home loan interest, HRA). We calculate your tax both ways and recommend the lower one before filing.

Can you help if I've received an income tax notice?

Yes. Share the notice and we'll explain what it means, what's being asked, and draft and file the response within the deadline.

Do you work with clients outside Karnataka?

Yes — we serve clients anywhere in India through phone, WhatsApp and video calls, with documents shared securely online.

ITR guide

Which ITR form should you file?

A quick overview of the income tax return forms. Filing the wrong form can make your return "defective", so check before you file.

FormWho can fileTypical income covered
ITR-1
Sahaj		Resident individuals with total income up to ₹50 lakhSalary or pension, one house property, interest and other sources, small agricultural income
ITR-2Individuals & HUFs without business incomeCapital gains, more than one house property, foreign assets or income, income above ₹50 lakh
ITR-3Individuals & HUFs with business or professional incomeProprietorship business, professional practice, F&O / intraday trading, partner's share from a firm
ITR-4
Sugam		Resident individuals, HUFs and firms (not LLPs) with income up to ₹50 lakhPresumptive income under sections 44AD, 44ADA or 44AE
ITR-5Partnership firms, LLPs, AOPs, BOIsBusiness income of the entity
ITR-6Companies (other than those claiming charitable exemption)Corporate income
ITR-7Trusts, institutions, political parties and similar bodiesIncome of charitable / religious trusts and specified institutions

Eligibility conditions change from year to year. The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026, so forms and section numbers may change for later years. We confirm the correct form for your year before filing.
